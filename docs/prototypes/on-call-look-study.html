    What to react to

    Choices in this draft

    • Service colours sit at one lightness and low chroma, so no service shouts. State (selected, error, drop target) is carried by outlines, never by fill.
    • Warm amber is the only glow on the page. The incident pulse is a dusty coral, not brand red, so it reads as "open" rather than "alarm".
    • The map and the success swirl keep a dark sky in the light theme too; the trace view follows the theme.
