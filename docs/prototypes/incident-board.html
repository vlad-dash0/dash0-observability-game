Prototype notes

  • Same rules: the scenario, budget, views and hypotheses come from levels/who-is-talking.mjs; openView, canAnswer, answer and budgetLeft are ported from lib/widgets/incident.mjs. The one addition is cluesOpened, which canAnswer now uses, so "Clues found" and the lock read the same count (as #143 asks).
  • Penalties as in incident-view.mjs: first open of a misleading view, the open that spends the last of the budget, a wrong pick. Repeats are free. Stars use lib/scoring.mjs (penalties plus hints, 5 stars max).
